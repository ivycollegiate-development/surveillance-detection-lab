# Incident Interview Notes

Transcribed from statements taken after the incident. Treat these as **claims
by the people who made them** — verify each one against the data.

---

## Statement 1 — M. Osei, Assistant Principal

> "I left at 23:45 on the 28th. I was the last one out of the main office. I
> remember locking up because I always do the last sweep. I don't think anyone
> else was in the building — I didn't see anyone, though."

*Note: the sweep includes the staff room. The sweep is not recorded anywhere.*

## Statement 2 — R. Alvarez, Librarian

> "I reported my badge lost on the 24th. I turned it in at the office, but they
> said they'd order a replacement. I never got one. I was still using the old
> one to get into the library because otherwise I couldn't get my books. I know
> that was wrong."

## Statement 3 — Facilities manager

> "The south gate was propped open all day because of the delivery. That
> happens. There's no camera on it — sorry, I know, it's on the list. I think
> there's a work order in for that camera but nobody ever signed it off. We've
> been meaning to. The stairwell has never had a camera. I wouldn't know where
> to put one, honestly, it's all plaster and conduit up there."

## Statement 4 — Groundskeeper

> "I was on the lot until about 9, then I went home. I parked under the tree
> on the far side, away from the entrance. I didn't see anything, and I wouldn't
> have seen much from there anyway, it's dark on that side. There's a light by
> the west gate but the far side of the lot has had a burned-out light since
> last year."

## Statement 5 — Police incident report (excerpt)

> "Victim reports a laptop removed from an unlocked staff room overnight.
> Forced entry not established. No suspects identified. Recovered item: none.
> Referral made for review of access control procedures."

## Statement 6 — IT technician

> "Nothing on the servers was touched, as far as I can tell. I checked the logs
> this morning. The laptop was a local machine though, it wasn't backed up to
> the server, so whatever was on the disk is just gone."

## Open questions nobody answered

- The staff room was unlocked. Nobody knows when, or by whom.
- No physical evidence was recovered. No tool marks, no prints mentioned.
- The badge log shows swipes. It does not show who made them.
- The incident was *reported*, not *detected*. Nobody received an alert.
